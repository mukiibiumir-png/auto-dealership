About AutoHub Uganda

Your trusted partner for quality cars and motorcycles across Uganda and East Africa.

Driven by trust, built for you.

AutoHub Uganda was founded with a simple mission: make buying and selling vehicles transparent, safe and stress-free for everyone. We understand that a car or motorcycle is more than a purchase — it's an investment in your daily life, your business, or your passion.

Our platform connects serious buyers with verified sellers, offering detailed listings, clear specifications and direct communication channels. Whether you're looking for a family SUV, a commuter bike or a premium sedan, our team is here to guide you every step of the way.

We are proudly Ugandan, serving customers across Kampala, Entebbe, Jinja, Mbarara and beyond. Our reach extends throughout East Africa, helping you find the right vehicle wherever you are.
